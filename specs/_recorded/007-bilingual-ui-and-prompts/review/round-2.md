#### [UNSTATED_ASSUMPTION] ADVISORY

- Location: plan.md § Live-tier risk; decision-log [11] *(carried from round 1, unaddressed)*
- Issue: the function-word assertion still contradicts its own exclusion rule and still leaves matching undefined. The English list still holds `problem`, which is `Problem` in German, and the drafted German system template in § The prompt templates asks for "das Problem, das es löst" — so a *correct* German question is likely to score an English hit while the rule claims "Content words that are near-identical in both languages are excluded on purpose". - Fix: In plan.md § Live-tier risk and decision-log [11], remove `problem` from the English list and add `you`, `solve` and `it`; state that matching is case-insensitive whole-word; and state that the score counts distinct list entries present, not total occurrences.
