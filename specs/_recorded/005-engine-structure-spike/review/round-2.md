## Requirement Quality

#### [AMBIGUOUS_REQUIREMENT] BLOCKER

- Location: `plan.md:207` (§ Decision › The decision rule, condition 1); `:169` (§ The four evaluation axes › Axis 1, the `session.json` paragraph); checked against `specs/roadmap.md:48`, `packages/core/src/interview/state.ts:6-8`, `packages/server/src/adapters/session-store/filesystem-session-store.ts:342-350`
- Issue: condition 1 is the decision rule's overriding gate — "A LangGraph channel blob on disk ends the case regardless of every other number, because it collides with a guardrail already `fest`" — and it carries two defects, one of which makes it unfalsifiable and the other of which makes it wrong.

  First, half the condition cannot come out either way. It requires arm B to keep "`InterviewState` v1 **with `schemaVersion`** as the on-disk shape of `session.json`". `schemaVersion` is not part of the domain state; it is an envelope field the store writes itself. `filesystem-session-store.ts:342-350`'s `toEnvelope` sets `schemaVersion: SCHEMA_VERSION` unconditionally and passes `state` through untouched, and the envelope declares `readonly state: unknown` (`:179`) with the comment "The store validates the envelope it owns and never the state it does not" (`:359`). Both arms drive `createFilesystemSessionStore` per § Both arms sit on the same real store, so both produce a `session.json` carrying `schemaVersion` whatever `state` holds. Task 10 checking the condition literally passes the gate on evidence no measurement could have changed — precisely what the subsection's own "Unreachable conditions are recorded, not counted" clause exists to catch, applied to the condition that overrides all others.

  Second, the guardrail cited does not say what the condition says. `specs/roadmap.md:48` reads "Jede persistierte Session trägt ab dem ersten Schreiben ein `schemaVersion`-Feld" — a requirement on the envelope, not on `TState`. `packages/core/src/interview/state.ts:6-8` states the distinction in the repository's own words: "The session store's `schemaVersion` is unaffected — it versions the on-disk envelope, not the domain state it wraps." A LangGraph channel blob written under `state` therefore carries `schemaVersion` and breaches no `fest` guardrail. The real grounds for objecting to a channel blob are elsewhere and weaker as stated: `specs/mission.md`'s readable-session promise, which `:169` cites correctly, and the `TState`-growth rationale in `:48`'s own second sentence ("`TState` wächst über M2 → M8 → M9 → M14"). Neither is a guardrail whose text a channel blob violates.

  Consequence: the same `session.json` supports both verdicts — gate passed (`schemaVersion` is present) and gate failed (`state` is not an `InterviewState`) — and the stated justification supports neither. A task-10 author who wants the hand-rolled answer can end the LangGraph case by naming a `fest` guardrail that was not breached, inside an ADR recorded `Accepted` that § Impact (`:246`) calls irreversible in practice. That is round 1's complaint surviving inside the fix, in the one condition that outranks every measurement.
- Fix: In `plan.md:207`, rewrite condition 1 to state a measurable predicate and the ground that actually supports it: "**Axis 4, gate.** `session.json`'s `state` field round-trips as `InterviewState` v1 — `{ turns: Turn[] }`, each turn's `status`, `question`, and `askedAt` (plus `answer` and `answeredAt` when answered) readable without LangGraph — by a mechanism task 5 implemented and recorded. A LangGraph channel blob under `state` ends the case regardless of every other number. The ground is `specs/mission.md`'s readable-session promise and the `TState`-growth rationale in `specs/roadmap.md:48`, **not** the `schemaVersion` field: `createFilesystemSessionStore` writes `schemaVersion` into the envelope for both arms regardless of what `state` holds (`packages/server/src/adapters/session-store/filesystem-session-store.ts:342-350`), so it discriminates nothing." Delete "with `schemaVersion`" from the condition's first sentence. Apply the same correction at `:169`: replace "a direct collision with a fixed guardrail" with "a collision with `specs/mission.md`'s readable-session promise and with the `TState`-growth rationale the `On-Disk-Session-Schema` guardrail states — not with that guardrail's `schemaVersion` requirement, which the envelope satisfies for both arms".
